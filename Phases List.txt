This is a mandatory special phase.

Implement

Supabase RPC
     ↓
PostgreSQL function
     ↓
transaction

The approval must atomically:

verify admin
↓
verify request
↓
verify Pending
↓
create leave_approval
↓
update leave_requests
↓
create/update attendance
↓
COMMIT

Mandatory because

Your schema explicitly requires leave approval to be transactional.

Without this, you could end up with:
